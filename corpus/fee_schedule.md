## Part D: Thesis and Dissertation Submission Deadlines

| Semester of Intended Award | Thesis/Dissertation Submission Deadline | Late Submission Surcharge |
|---|---|---|
| Autumn (December award) | 15 October | $95 surcharge applies; subject to examiner availability |
| Spring (May award) | 15 February | $95 surcharge applies; subject to examiner availability |
| Summer (August award) | 15 June | $95 surcharge applies; subject to examiner availability |

Students who miss the thesis submission deadline without an approved extension will not be examined in the intended semester and must reapply for examination in the following semester. The $95 late submission surcharge is charged regardless of whether the thesis is ultimately accepted in that examination cycle.

## Part F: Payment Methods and Procedures

Tuition and fees may be paid by:

- Online via the student portal (credit/debit card, bank transfer)
- In person at the Bursar's Office (cheque, money order, or cash)
- International wire transfer (additional bank fee may apply)

Students experiencing financial hardship should contact the Financial Aid Office at least six weeks before the applicable deadline to discuss options. The University does not guarantee emergency funding, but a limited Emergency Bursary Fund is available for eligible students in crisis.

---

## Part G: Consequences of Non-Payment

A student whose account remains unpaid after all late payment fees have been applied may be:

1. Placed on a Financial Hold (blocking registration, transcripts, and participation in Commencement).
2. Reported to a collections agency for accounts outstanding beyond ninety days.
3. Administratively deregistered from current courses at the discretion of the Bursar in consultation with the Registrar.

The University reserves the right to withhold degree conferral until all outstanding balances are cleared.
